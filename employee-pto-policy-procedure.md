# Employee Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy and Procedure

## Purpose

This policy and procedure establishes guidelines for using paid time off (PTO) and provides instructions for requesting and managing PTO.

## Scope

This policy applies to all full-time employees eligible to accrue and use PTO.

## Policy

Eligible employees may use accrued PTO for vacation, personal time, illness, appointments, and other absences from work.

Employees are responsible for ensuring they have sufficient available PTO before requesting time off. Scheduled PTO should be requested in advance and is subject to manager approval based on staffing and business needs.

Employees should not consider a PTO request approved until they receive confirmation from their manager.

## PTO Accrual and Balances

- Full-time employees begin accruing PTO on their first day of employment.
- PTO accrues each pay period.
- Employees can view their available PTO balance in the employee system.
- Displayed balances may not reflect requests that have been submitted but have not yet been processed.

## Responsibilities

### Employees

Employees are responsible for:

- Monitoring their available PTO balance.
- Submitting scheduled PTO requests in advance.
- Waiting for manager approval before considering requested time off approved.
- Following departmental call-out procedures for unexpected absences.

### Managers

Managers are responsible for:

- Reviewing employee PTO requests.
- Considering staffing needs, business requirements, available PTO, and other employee requests.
- Responding to PTO requests in a timely manner.

## Request Scheduled PTO

Submit scheduled PTO requests at least two weeks before the requested time off whenever possible.

To request PTO:

1. Sign in to the employee system.
2. Select **Time Off** from the main menu.
3. Select **Request Time Off**.
4. Enter the date or dates requested.
5. Select **PTO** as the time-off type.
6. Enter the number of PTO hours requested.
7. Review the request for accuracy.
8. Select **Submit**.

Your request is sent to your manager for review. Do not consider the time off approved until you receive confirmation of approval.

## Check the Status of a PTO Request

To check the status of a submitted request:

1. Sign in to the employee system.
2. Select **Time Off**.
3. View your submitted PTO requests.
4. Review the request status.

| Status | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | The request is awaiting manager review. |
| **Approved** | The manager approved the request. |
| **Denied** | The manager did not approve the request. |

If your request is denied, contact your manager if you have questions or want to discuss alternative dates.

## Unplanned Absences

Employees who are unable to report to work as scheduled should notify their manager as soon as possible.

When reporting an unplanned absence:

1. Contact your manager using your department's established call-out method.
2. Provide your expected absence or anticipated return date, if known.
3. Submit the appropriate PTO request in the employee system as soon as reasonably possible.

Employees must follow any additional attendance or notification requirements established by their department.

## PTO Carryover

Unused PTO may be carried over to the following year, subject to company carryover limits.

Employees are responsible for monitoring their PTO balances and should review available balances before the end of the calendar year.

PTO that exceeds the allowable carryover limit may be forfeited.

## Separation from Employment

Treatment of unused PTO upon separation from employment is subject to company policy and applicable state law.

Employees should contact Human Resources with questions regarding unused PTO balances or final pay.

## Questions

For questions about PTO eligibility, accrual, balances, or policy requirements, contact Human Resources.

For questions about a specific PTO request or scheduling needs, contact your manager.
