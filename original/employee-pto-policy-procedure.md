# Employee Paid Time Off Policy and Procedure

Employees are provided paid time off (PTO) to use for vacation, personal time, illness, appointments, and other absences from work. PTO should be scheduled in advance whenever possible. Employees are responsible for making sure they have enough PTO available before requesting time away from work.

Full-time employees begin accruing PTO on their first day of employment. PTO is accrued each pay period. Employees can view their current PTO balance in the employee system. The balance displayed may not include PTO requests that have been submitted but have not yet been processed.

Employees who want to take scheduled PTO should submit a request through the employee system. To request PTO, log in to the employee system and select **Time Off** from the main menu. Select **Request Time Off**, enter the date or dates being requested, select **PTO** as the time-off type, enter the number of hours requested, and submit the request.

PTO requests should normally be submitted at least two weeks before the requested time off when possible. Employees should not assume that a request has been approved until they receive confirmation from their manager.

After an employee submits a request, the employee's manager reviews it. Approval is based on available PTO, staffing needs, business requirements, and other employee requests. Managers should review requests and respond in a timely manner.

Employees can check the status of a submitted request by returning to the **Time Off** section of the employee system and viewing their submitted requests. Requests may display as Pending, Approved, or Denied.

If a request is denied, employees should contact their manager if they have questions or need to discuss alternative dates.

Employees who need unexpected time off because of illness or an emergency should contact their manager as soon as possible and follow their department's normal call-out procedures. Employees should not use the standard advance PTO request process when immediate notification is required.

Employees may carry over unused PTO into the following calendar year, subject to the company's annual carryover limit. PTO above the allowable limit may be forfeited if it is not used before the end of the year.

When employment ends, unused PTO will be handled according to applicable company policy and state law.

Questions about PTO balances, accrual, eligibility, or other PTO-related issues should be directed to Human Resources.
